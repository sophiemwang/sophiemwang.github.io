Beyond the Star Rating: Sentiment and Trend Analysis of Written Restaurant Reviews

Sophie Wang

Advisor: Stephen Slade

Abstract

This project investigates the intersection of professional food criticism and public sentiment by analyzing over 41,000 Yelp reviews across fifty New York City restaurants reviewed by New York Times food critic Pete Wells. A custom Python pipeline was developed to process and analyze sentiment in these reviews using a domain-specific sentiment classifier. This model, which builds upon the VADER framework and incorporates tailored lexicon implementations for restaurant-specific language, assigns each review a polarity score and sentiment label. The pipeline enables temporal comparisons of sentiment before and after the publication of Wells’ reviews, offering a unique lens into the potential influence of expert critique. The study's results find that Wells’ “fair” rating coincides with a statistically significant drop in Yelp review volume, suggesting reduced diner engagement rather than heightened diner negative criticism. However, across all other categories (the other rating types given by Wells, cuisine types, and pricing tiers), the impact of Wells’ reviews on review sentiment or volume was not statistically significant. Results also show that the domain-specific sentiment model more accurately aligns with star ratings, underscoring the importance of tailored NLP tools. In addition to sentiment tracking, this study introduces an interactive web-based tool that visualizes the most frequently mentioned words in reviews by sentiment and time frame. This tool can help businesses identify shifts in customer priorities, such as increased mentions of a particular dish or pricing, and compare language usage across time windows. It offers a practical and accessible way for restaurants to analyze evolving themes in customer feedback and adjust strategy accordingly. Overall, the methodology and findings have implications beyond restaurant critiques, offering a framework for analyzing the effects of professional opinion in any domain where crowd-sourced feedback can interact with expert reviews; the webpage reinforces this practical value, making sentiment insights actionable for both industry stakeholders and the broader public.
